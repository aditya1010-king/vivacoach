Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

VivaCoach (viva.akone.xyz) is run by AK ONE, an independent student founder in India. This page explains what we collect when you use VivaCoach, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address from Google sign-in or the email sign-in link.
  • Your course: the university, course or branch and semester you enter, and the subjects you add.
  • Your practice: the answers you type or speak, the marks and feedback you receive, viva dates you set, and questions you report.
  • Payments: the pass you bought, the amount and the payment reference you send us. We never see or store your bank, UPI PIN or card details; you pay through your own UPI app.

When you use the microphone, your browser turns speech into text. VivaCoach receives only the text, never the audio.

Google user data

When you choose "Continue with Google", VivaCoach asks Google only for basic sign-in information: your name, email address and profile picture (the "openid", "email" and "profile" permissions). We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

  • How we use it: only to create your VivaCoach account, sign you in, show your name in the app, match your UPI payments to your account and reply to your messages.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share your Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. It is stored by our database provider (Supabase) only to run VivaCoach.
  • Storage and deletion: it is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you choose Account → Delete my account, or within 30 days of emailing us to ask.
  • Revoking access: you can also remove VivaCoach's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

VivaCoach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Why we use it

  • To mark your answers and show you which topics to practise again.
  • To show subjects and questions that classmates in the same course have added.
  • To manage free sessions and paid passes, and to answer your messages and refund requests.
  • To fix wrong questions and improve VivaCoach.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: stores your account and practice data.
  • Google (Gemini API): receives the question and your answer text to produce marks and feedback, and course details to suggest subjects and write questions.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website.
  • Google sign-in: confirms who you are when you choose "Continue with Google".

These services may process data outside India.

What other students can see

Subjects and topics you add, and the questions written for them, can be seen and used by other students in the same course. Your name, email, answers and marks are never shown to other students.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account anytime from Account → Delete my account, which removes your answers, marks, viva dates and passes immediately. Subjects you added stay available for your classmates, without your name.

Your choices

You can see your data in the app, delete your account yourself, or email us to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. VivaCoach is meant for college students aged 18 or over.
